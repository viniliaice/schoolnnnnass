# Design: Family ID Generator for Non-Bus Students (MBK International School)

## Problem Statement

At dismissal, walker and private-car students are matched to their families **from memory** by gate staff. The memory is a single person's (Umal Kharye Xuseen). When memory fails, staff call parents to ask "are you picking up X?", kids wait, parents circle the block — and in at least one real incident, a **wrong parent took a kid** and it was discovered after the fact. The school already tracks who is not on the bus (the `nb`/empty rows in the Google Sheet's Bus column), but that data is never turned into a matching tool at the gate. Bus students have bus numbers; non-bus students have nothing.

## Demand Evidence

- **A wrong parent actually took a child** — "wrong perrent talking the kids dicvoireced" (user, verbatim). This is the panic-when-it-breaks level of demand: a safety failure, not an inconvenience.
- **Named users who live this daily:** Umal Kharye Xuseen (gate lead), with assistants Maxamed Aden and Abdurahman Aw Nuux.
- **The status quo fallback is a phone call** — "Umal having to call a parent to ask 'are you picking up X?'" (user, verbatim). Each such call is minutes of delay per family, every day.
- **The demand list already exists in maintained data:** the per-student Google Sheet table with columns `numberGov | Bus | Grade | Name | SECOND NUMBER`, where `nb` or empty in the Bus column marks walker/private-car students.

## Status Quo

1. Bus students: organized — bus numbers in the Google Sheet.
2. Non-bus students (walkers, private-car): matched by gate staff memory; fallback is calling the parent's SECOND NUMBER from the sheet.
3. Data lives in Google Sheets, per student, updated by the school. It is not in the app's students table, and nothing at the gate consumes it.
4. Cost: minutes per family at peak pickup, cognitive load on three named people, and one discovered wrong-parent handoff. In a school context that incident is one near-miss away from something worse.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

**Target human:** Umal Kharye Xuseen — the person physically at the gate at dismissal. Assistants Maxamed Aden and Abdurahman Aw Nuux. Their day is ruined by slow, uncertain matching; their responsibility is that no kid leaves with the wrong adult.

**Narrowest wedge (user-selected, D5 → B):** the full gate loop, staged:
- **M1:** family IDs exist + printable cards + gate lookup list (fixes the wrong-parent moment immediately)
- **M2:** gate screen — staff enter/scan a family ID and instantly see the kids (memory becomes backup)
- **M3:** parents see their family ID in the parent portal

## Constraints

- React 19 + Vite + Tailwind 4 + Supabase (PostgreSQL, RLS, Edge Functions); TanStack React Query; single-file build via vite-plugin-singlefile.
- App already has: `@react-pdf/renderer` (printable PDFs), `xlsx` (bulk import), a BulkUpload admin flow, a parent portal, and an RLS pattern.
- Google Sheet is the **maintained source of truth** for transport data; the app imports it (user's explicit revision: mirror it into students-table columns).
- Gate environment: mobile phone in hand, possibly slow/unreliable network at the gate — typing a 3–5 digit ID must be faster than asking "are you picking up X?".
- Safety-critical: any release-verification feature must default to *deny* (never suggest a match on partial input).
- Language reality: school community is Soomaali-speaking; docs exist in English + Soomaali. Gate UI should stay minimal and visual (big numbers, no prose).

## Premises

1. **The family is the unit of identity.** Siblings share one family ID; the ID matches a family to its kids at the gate. (User confirmed — "a id generator for my families".)
2. **Grouping key:** shared phone (the sheet's SECOND NUMBER / parent phone) where `parentId` isn't linked yet, with a manual override UI for edge cases (two families sharing a phone, one family with multiple phones).
3. **Data lives in the app, not just the sheet.** Add columns to the students table: `govId` (the sheet's numberGov) and `transport` (bus number, or `nb`/empty = non-bus). Google Sheet imports via the existing BulkUpload/xlsx path. IDs are generated for non-bus families only; bus students keep bus numbers. *(Revised by user: "add coulmn in the students table Gov-id and Bus/nb".)*
4. **The gate flow replaces memory-first:** staff enter or scan the family ID → see the kids → release. Memory stays as backup, never primary.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Minimal — schema + generator + printable cards
- Add `govId` + `transport` columns; admin "Family IDs" page that assigns `MBK-####` per family; printable PDF cards + gate lookup list. No gate screen.
- Effort: S (human ~1–2 days / CC ~20 min). Risk: Low. Reuses: BulkUpload, xlsx, react-pdf, ManageStudents.
- Pros: ships in a day; kills the wrong-parent moment with a laminated card. Cons: Umal still reads cards and names manually; no in-app lookup; cards can be forgotten/lost.

### Approach B: Full gate — A + gate screen + parent portal *(chosen, revised: QR on every family card)*
- Everything in A, plus a mobile gate screen with **three input paths**: type the 3–5 digit ID (big keypad), scan the QR with a phone camera, or scan with a hardware barcode scanner (scanners emulate a keyboard — the typed input path covers them for free). QR is printed on every family card (and optionally a windshield placard). Parents see their family ID in the portal.
- Effort: M (human ~3–5 days / CC ~45 min). Risk: Med. Reuses: everything in A + existing portal layouts + RLS patterns.
- Pros: complete loop — ID is verifiable in-app in seconds; scanning removes transcription errors; portal gives parents a canonical copy of their ID; release log (who left with whom) becomes possible. Cons: more moving parts; gate screen needs mobile access decisions; QR generation + camera decode add a small library dependency.

### Approach C: Placard-first — QR car placards + camera scanning
- Same IDs, but the primary artifact is a windshield/rearview-mirror placard with a big `MBK-042` and QR; gate staff scan with a phone camera; grade color-bands on cards.
- Effort: M (human ~3–4 days / CC ~40 min). Risk: Med.
- Pros: fastest possible gate throughput; QR removes transcription errors. Cons: depends on camera+QR reliability at the gate; placards are an extra physical artifact to print and enforce.

**Recommendation: B**, staged M1 → M2 → M3, because it fixes the safety failure at M1 while leaving the gate screen (the part that actually replaces Umal's memory) as an immediate follow-up. C's placard idea is a cheap add-on to B later (a QR on the card costs nothing extra once the generator exists).

## Recommended Approach

Approach B, delivered in three milestones:

- **M1 — Generator + print (days 1–2):**
  - Migration: `students.govId TEXT`, `students.transport TEXT` (bus number | `nb` | empty), `students.familyId TEXT` (+ index on familyId).
  - BulkUpload CSV mapping gains `Gov-id` and `Bus/nb` columns.
  - Admin page "Family IDs": button **Generate** → groups non-bus students (transport = `nb`/empty) by family (parentId → else shared SECOND NUMBER phone) → assigns `MBK-####` per family → writes `familyId` to each student. Idempotent: existing IDs preserved on re-run; manual override to merge/split families.
  - Print: PDF via @react-pdf/renderer — one card per family (big family ID **plus QR code encoding the ID**, student names + grades, parent phones) + a gate lookup list sorted by family ID. QR generated as a PNG data URL (`qrcode` lib) and embedded in the PDF, so the card is scannable by phone camera or hardware scanner.
- **M2 — Gate screen (days 3–4):**
  - Mobile-friendly admin/supervisor route (e.g. `/gate`): **big keypad + input for family ID** (hardware barcode scanners work through this input — they type the code), **camera QR scan** (`html5-qrcode` or `@zxing/browser`), shows matched students (name, grade, transport, parent phone). **Lookup-only in M2** — the "release" tap and handoff log are deferred to TODOS.md per the CEO review. Scan path must work in daylight with a phone camera. NOT-FOUND lookups are audit-logged.
- **M3 — Parent portal (day 5):**
  - Parent dashboard shows their family ID and their kids' IDs; printable reminder card link.

## Open Questions

1. **Family grouping ground truth:** do siblings reliably share the same SECOND NUMBER in the sheet? (Assignment #1 answers this.)
2. **Gate network:** is there usable wifi/mobile signal at the gate, or does M2 need an offline-tolerant mode (e.g. cache the lookup table on the phone)?
3. **ID format:** `MBK-####` vs short numeric (e.g. `0421`) for typing speed on a phone keypad. Short numeric wins for gate speed; prefix helps printing.
4. **Card issuance:** who prints — school office printer (PDF) or parents self-print? Affects card size (ID-card vs A4 slip) and QR size/contrast for scanning.
5. **QR scanning reality at the gate:** daylight glare on phone cameras, scanner hardware availability (does MBK own a barcode scanner?), and whether a windshield placard version of the card is wanted for car-line pickup.
6. **Bus families later?** The gate screen could eventually cover bus students too, but v1 is non-bus only per the user's ask.
7. **Release log scope:** does the school want an audit trail (who released which kid to whom, when) from day one, or defer?

## Success Criteria

- Zero wrong-parent handoffs for non-bus students after M1 goes live (measured by incident report).
- Umal stops calling parents to ask "are you picking up X?" for non-bus families (self-reported; count per week → target 0).
- 100% of non-bus families hold a family ID within 1 week of M1.
- Gate lookup time < 10 seconds per family at M2 via **any** of the three input paths: typed ID, phone-camera QR scan, hardware scanner.
- QR on the printed card scans successfully with a phone camera in daylight conditions (tested on card stock before mass printing).
- Re-running Generate never duplicates or reassigns existing family IDs (idempotency test).

## Distribution Plan

Web app — the existing Vite build + deployment pipeline for MBK covers this (single-file build, Supabase-hosted). No new distribution channel. Migration is applied via the repo's Supabase migrations folder.

## Dependencies

- Supabase migration: `students.govId`, `students.transport`, `students.familyId` (+ indexes, RLS read rules for staff roles).
- BulkUpload: CSV header mapping for `Gov-id`, `Bus/nb`.
- @react-pdf/renderer already installed (cards/lookup PDF).
- QR: `qrcode` (PNG data-URL generation for embedding in the PDF) at M1; `html5-qrcode` or `@zxing/browser` (camera decode) at M2. Both are small, well-maintained libraries; hardware scanners need no library (keyboard emulation).
- Design docs pattern: existing repo `DESIGN-*.md` convention; downstream skills will find this doc.
