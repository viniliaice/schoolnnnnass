## Vision

### 10x Check
One dismissal system instead of a memory plus a spreadsheet. Every family (bus and non-bus) has an ID; the gate screen is the only tool at the door; every release is a recorded, visible event. The client-side generator (Approach A) is the foundation; the expansions accepted below are what stack on it without new plumbing.

### Platonic Ideal
A parent's car pulls into the line — Umal points the phone at the placard, beep, kids' names appear, green RELEASE tap, done. A walker scans her lanyard — beep — logged out with her family. Parents see releases remotely. Zero shouting, zero memory, zero "are you picking up X?". Gate staff's job changes from remembering every family to watching one screen.

## Accepted Scope (added to this plan)
- Generator assigns family IDs to **all** families (not only nb/empty). Transport column still prints on card (bus number | WALKER | CAR).
- Card PDF gains three layouts: pocket card, lanyard card (walkers), windshield placard (car line). Same QR, different size/orientation.
- Gate screen UI in Soomaali with English toggle.
