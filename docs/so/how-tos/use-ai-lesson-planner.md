# Sida loo Isticmaalo Qorsheeye Casharrada AI

Samee qorshayaal cashar, hel jawaab celin AI, oo u gudbi kormeeraha dib u eegista.

## Waxyaabaha loo baahan yahay

- Door macallin
- Fasallo iyo maadooyin la qoondeeyay

## Abuurista qorshaha casharka

1. Guji **Lesson Plans** bar-ka bidix.
2. Guji **Create Plan**.
3. Buuxi:
   - **Title:** tusaale "Usbuuca 12 - Jajabka"
   - **Subject:** ka xulo maadooyinkaaga
   - **Class:** ka xulo fasalladaaga
   - **Week Label:** tusaale "Usbuuca 12"
4. Guji **Save**.

## Ku darista xilliyada (periods)

Qorshaha casharku wuxuu daboolayaa usbuuc dugsi (Sabti ilaa Arbaco, 5 maalmood).

1. Guji maalin (Sabti, Axad, iwm).
2. Xilli kasta, buuxi:
   - **Period Number:** 1-6
   - **Topic:** waxaad baraysid
   - **Objective:** ujeeddada barashada
   - **Activities:** waxa ardaydu qaban doonaan
   - **Slide Number:** tixraac (ikhtiyaar)
   - **Is Free:** haddii xilligu xor yahay
3. Guji **Save Periods**.

### Faahfaahinta xilliga

Xilli kasta, waxaad ku dari kartaa hawlo qaabeysan:

1. Guji **Add Activity** xilliga.
2. Buuxi:
   - **Activity:** sharaxaad
   - **Time:** muddo (tusaale "15 min")
   - **Resource:** agab loo baahan yahay
   - **Place:** goobta (tusaale "Fasalka", "Lab")

## Gudbinta dib u eegista AI

1. Ka dib markaad buuxiso dhamaan xilliyada, guji **Submit for Review**.
2. Nidaamku wuxuu u diraa qorshahaaga edge function-ka AI.
3. Sug dib u eegista (caadi ahaan 10-30 ilbiriqsi).

### Buundooyinka dib u eegista AI

AI-ga wuxuu qiimeeyaa qorshahaaga 10 qaybood:

| Qaybta | Waxay qiyaastaa |
|--------|-----------------|
| Learning Objectives | Caddaynta, gaar ahaanta, qiyaasanka |
| Lesson Structure | Qulqulka, wakhtiga, isku xirka |
| Student Engagement | Barashada firfircoon, fursadaha ka qayb galka |
| Teaching Strategies | Kala duwanaanshaha, habboonaanta |
| Differentiation | La qabsiga ardayda kala duwan |
| Assessment Methods | Sida barashada loo hubiyo |
| Curriculum Alignment | Isku habboonaanta heerarka |
| Classroom Management | Anshaxa, maamulka wakhtiga |
| Resources Materials | Habboonaanta, helitaanka |
| Overall Quality | Qiimeyn guud |

Qayb kastaa waxay heshaa buundo (0-100) iyo sharaxaad.

### Heerarka

| Boqolleyda | Heer |
|-----------|------|
| 90-100 | Aad u fiican |
| 80-89 | Wanaagsan |
| 70-79 | Fican |
| 60-69 | U Baahan Hagaajin |
| 0-59 | U Baahan Dib u Qorid |

## Dib u eegista kormeeraha

Ka dib dib u eegista AI, qorshuhu wuxuu u tagayaa kormeerahaaga:

1. Kormeeruhu wuxuu arkaa buundooyinka AI iyo soo koobista.
2. Wuxuu ku darayaa faallooyinkiisa.
3. Wuxuu ansixinayaa ama codsanayaa dib u hagaajin.
4. Haddii dib u hagaajin la codsado, waad tafatirtaa oo dib u gudbisaa.

## Xaqiijin

- Heerka qorshuhu wuu isbeddelaa: `draft` → `submitted` → `in_review` → `approved`/`rejected`
- Buundooyinka AI waxay ku muugdaan qorshaha
- Kormeerayaashu waxay arkaan qorshayaasha sugaya safkooda

## Cillad saarista

**Dhibaato:** Dib u eegista AI way fashilantay.
**Xal:** Hubi in dhamaan xilliyadu leeyihiin mawduuc iyo hawlo. AI-ga wuxuu u baahan yahay wax uu ku qiimeeyo.

**Dhibaato:** Ma gudbin karo dib u eegista.
**Xal:** Hubi in qorshuhu leeyahay ugu yaraan hal xilli oo nuxur leh.

**Dhibaato:** Buundooyinku hooseeyaan.
**Xal:** Akhri talooyinka AI-ga ee hagaajinta. Diyaari ujeedooyin cad, hawlo kala duwan, iyo habab qiimeyn oo wanaagsan.
