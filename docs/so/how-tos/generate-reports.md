# Sida loo Soo Saaro Warbixinnada Ardayda

Nidaamku wuxuu soo saaraa saddex nooc oo warbixin ah: bishii, xilliyeed (midterm), iyo dhammaystir (final). Mid kasta wuxuu u soo koobaa xogta imtixaanka si kala duwan.

## Waxyaabaha loo baahan yahay

- Natiijooyinka imtixaanka la galay oo la ansixiyay
- Sannad dugsiyeed iyo xilliyo la dejiyay
- Miisaanka buundooyinka la qeexay

## Warbixinnada bishii

Waxay tusaysaa hal bil oo imtixaan ah maaddiiba.

1. Guji **Monthly Report** (waalid) ama **Exam Reports** (admin/macallin).
2. Xulo **Student** iyo **Month**.
3. Warbixintu waxay tusaysaa maaddiiba:
   - Buundo kasta oo imtixaan ah (CA, Homework, Classwork, Quiz, Attendance)
   - Celcelis buundo bisha
   - Celcelis fasalka isbarbardhig

### Sida celceliska bisha loo xisaabiyo

Celcelis Bishii = Wadarta dhamaan buundooyinka imtixaanka / Tirada imtixaannada

Imtixaan kastaa wuxuu si siman ugu kaa qayb qaataa celceliska bisha.

## Warbixinta xilliyeed (midterm)

Waxay isu geleysaa bilo badan oo xilli ah.

1. Guji **Midterm Report**.
2. Xulo **Student** iyo **Term**.
3. Warbixintu waxay tusaysaa:
   - Buundooyinka maaddiiba xilliga oo dhan
   - Darajada maadada (booska ardayda fasalka dhexdiisa)
   - Celcelis fasalka maaddiiba
   - Buundada ugu sarraysa fasalka
   - Darajada guud ardayda oo dhan

### Sida buundada xilliyeed loo xisaabiyo

Buundo Xilliyeed = Celcelis celcelisyada bisha xilliga

## Warbixinta dhammaystirka (final)

Waxay isku darsaa CA, midterm, iyo final leh miisaan la dejin karo.

1. Guji **Final Report**.
2. Xulo **Student** iyo **Term**.
3. Warbixintu waxay tusaysaa:
   - Celcelis CA (le'eg)
   - Buundo midterm (le'eg)
   - Buundo final (le'eg)
   - Wadarta buundada dhamaystirka
   - Darajada xarafka (A-F)
   - Gudbay/Dhicisoobay
   - Faallo macallin
   - Faallo maamule

### Sida buundada dhamaystirka loo xisaabiyo

Buundo Dhamaystir = (Celcelis CA × Miisaan CA) + (Midterm × Miisaan Midterm) + (Final × Miisaan Final)

Miisaanka caadiga: CA 30%, Midterm 30%, Final 40%.

### Miisaanka buundooyinka

| Buundo | Darajo | Faallo |
|--------|--------|--------|
| 90-100 | A | Aad u fiican |
| 80-89 | B | Wanaagsan |
| 70-79 | C | Fican |
| 60-69 | D | Gudbay |
| 0-59 | F | Dhicisoobay |

Heerka gudub: 60.

## Xaqiijin

- Warbixinnada bishii waxay cusboonaadaan si degdeg ah marka imtixaannada la ansixiyo
- Warbixinnada xilliyeed waxay dib u xisaabiyaan marka bil xilliga ka mid ah isbeddesho
- Warbixinnada dhamaystirka waxay muujiyaan miisaanka iyo buundooyinka ugu dambeeyay

## Cillad saarista

**Dhibaato:** Warbixintu ma tuso xog.
**Xal:** Hubi in imtixaannada ay jiraan ardayga la xulay, bisha/xilliga, oo la ansixiyay (status = "approved").

**Dhibaato:** Darajo khaldan warbixinta dhamaystirka.
**Xal:** Hubi in miisaanka Report Config uu isku darayo 100 iyo in miisaanka buundooyinka sax yahay.

**Dhibaato:** Darajada ardaygu khaldan.
**Xal:** Darajada waxaa loo xisaabiyaa ardayda fasalka la midka ah dhexdooda. Hubi `className`-ka ardayga.
