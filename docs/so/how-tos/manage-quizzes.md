# Sida loo Sameeyo oo loo Maamulo Su'aalaha

Dhis su'aalo leh ikhtiyaar iyo jawaab toos ah, ku daabac ardayda, oo buundo.

## Waxyaabaha loo baahan yahay

- Door macallin ama admin
- Fasallo iyo maadooyin la qoondeeyay

## Abuurista su'aalaha

1. Guji **Quizzes** bar-ka bidix.
2. Guji **Create Quiz**.
3. Buuxi:
   - **Title:** tusaale "Cutubka 5 Dib u Eegis"
   - **Class:** xulo fasalka
   - **Subject:** xulo maadada
   - **Description:** sharaxaad (ikhtiyaar)
   - **Open Date:** marka ardaydu billaabi karaan
   - **Due Date:** taariikhda dhamaynta
   - **Time Limit:** daqiiqado (tusaale 30)
   - **Question Order:** "created" (sida loo sameeyay) ama "randomized"
4. Guji **Save as Draft** ama **Publish**.

## Ku darista su'aalaha

1. Guji **Add Question**.
2. Dooro nooca su'aasha:

### Ikhtiyaar (Multiple choice)
- Geli **Prompt** (su'aasha)
- Ku dar ikhtiyaar (ugu yaraan 2):
  - **Label:** A, B, C, D
  - **Text:** jawaabta
- Calaamadee **Correct Answer**
- Deji **Points** (caadi ahaan 1)

### Jawaab toos ah (Direct answer)
- Geli **Prompt**
- Deji **Correct Answer** (buundo toos ah)
- Ikhtiyaar ahaan ku dar **Rubric** (buundo gacanta)
- Deji **Points**

3. Ku celi su'aal kasta.
4. Guji **Save**.

## Daabacaadda

- **Draft:** adiga oo keliya ayaa arki kara
- **Active:** ardaydu way qaadan karaan
- **Closed:** waa la xiray, natiijooyinka waa la arki karaa

Deji heerka **Active** marka diyaar u tahay ardayda.

## Buundada (grading)

1. Guji **Grade Quizzes** bar-ka bidix.
2. Xulo su'aalaha.
3. Iskuul kasta:
   - Dib u eeg jawaabaha ardayga
   - Ikhtiyaar: toos buundo, dib u eeg kuwa calaamadaysan
   - Jawaab toos: gacanta ku buundo adigoo isticmaalaya rubric
   - Geli **Points Earned** su'aal kasta
4. Guji **Save Grade**.

## Daawashada natiijooyinka

- Liiska su'aalaha wuxuu tusayaa tirooyinka iskuulka iyo celceliska
- Grade Quizzes wuxuu tusayaa jawaabaha arday kasta
- Waalidku wuxuu arkaa natiijooyinka su'aalaha carruurtooda

## Xaqiijin

- Ardaydu waxay arkaan su'aalaha inta u dhaxaysa taariikhda furitaanka iyo dhamaynta
- Buundooyinku waxay ku soo bixi warbixinnada buundada ka dib
- Su'aalaha wakhtiga leh way is-gudbiyaan markuu wakhtigu dhammoodo

## Cillad saarista

**Dhibaato:** Ardaydu ma arki karaan su'aalaha.
**Xal:** Hubi in heerku yahay "Active" iyo in maanta u dhaxayso taariikhda furitaanka iyo dhamaynta.

**Dhibaato:** Wakhtigu shaqeyn maayo.
**Xal:** Wakhtigu wuxuu u baahan yahay in ardaygu browser-ka furayahay. Haddii uu xiro oo dib u furo, wakhtigu wuu ka sii wadaa halkii uu ka tagay.

**Dhibaato:** Ma buundi karo su'aasha jawaabta tooska ah.
**Xal:** Rubric-ku wuxuu kaa caawinayaa buundada — jawaabta tooska ah waa gacanta lagu buundaa. Geli buundooyinka gacanta.
