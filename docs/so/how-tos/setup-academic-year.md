# Sida loo Sameeyo Sannad Dugsiyeed iyo Xilli

Kaabayasha taariikhda dugsiyada si imtixaannada, warbixinnada, iyo xaadirinta ugu xirnaadaan wakhtiga saxda ah.

## Waxyaabaha loo baahan yahay

- Door admin ah
- Helitaanka qaybta Academic

## Tallaabooyinka

### Abuur sannad dugsiyeed

1. Guji **Academic** bar-ka bidix.
2. Guji **Create Year**.
3. Buuxi:
   - **Name:** tusaale "2025-2026"
   - **Start Date:** maalinta koowaad ee sannadka
   - **End Date:** maalinta ugu dambaysa ee sannadka
   - **Is Current:** Gali ON (hal sannad oo keliya ayaa noqon kara current)
4. Guji **Save**.

### Abuur xilliyo sannadka dhexdiisa

1. Arag sannadka, guji **Add Term**.
2. Buuxi:
   - **Name:** tusaale "Term 1", "Term 2", "Term 3"
   - **Start Date:** maalinta koowaad ee xilliga
   - **End Date:** maalinta ugu dambaysa ee xilliga
   - **Is Current:** Gali ON xilliga firfircoon
   - **Months:** xulo bilaha uu xilligu daboolayo
3. Guji **Save**.

### Deji miisaanka buundooyinka

1. Qaybta Academic, raadso **Grade Scales**.
2. Qeex buundooyinka:

| Min | Max | Grade | Faallo | GPA |
|-----|-----|-------|--------|-----|
| 90 | 100 | A | Aad u fiican | 4.0 |
| 80 | 89 | B | Wanaagsan | 3.5 |
| 70 | 79 | C | Fican | 3.0 |
| 60 | 69 | D | Gudbay | 2.5 |
| 0 | 59 | F | Dhicisoobay | 0.0 |

3. Guji **Save**.

### Deji miisaanka warbixinta

1. Raadso **Report Config** qaybta Academic.
2. Deji miisaanka buundada dhamaystirka:
   - **CA Weight:** tusaale 30%
   - **Midterm Weight:** tusaale 30%
   - **Final Weight:** tusaale 40%
3. Miisaanku waa inuu isku daraa 100.
4. Guji **Save**.

## Xaqiijin

- Dashboard-ka wuxuu tusayaa sannadka iyo xilliga firfircoon.
- Macallimiintu waxay gelin karaan imtixaannada xilliga hadda jira oo keliya.
- Warbixinnadu waxay xisaabiyaan iyadoo la isticmaalayo miisaanka la dejiyay.

## Cillad saarista

**Dhibaato:** Macallimiintu ma gelin karaan imtixaannada bisha.
**Xal:** Hubi in bisha ku jirto liiska xilliga hadda.

**Dhibaato:** Warbixinnadu waxay tusayaan buundo khaldan.
**Xal:** Hubi in miisaanka buundooyinka sax yahay oo is dhaafin.

**Dhibaato:** Laba sannad oo current ah.
**Xal:** Hal sannad oo keliya ayaa noqon kara current — ka saar kan hore.
