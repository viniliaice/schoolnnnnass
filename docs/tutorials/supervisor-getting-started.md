# Tutorial: Supervisor Getting Started

This tutorial walks you through the supervisor workflow — from logging in to reviewing a lesson plan. You'll learn how to verify exam entries, monitor teacher progress, and approve lesson plans.

## What you'll need

- Supervisor credentials (email + password)
- A browser

## Step 1: Log in

1. Open the application URL.
2. Enter your supervisor email and password.
3. Click **Sign In**.

You'll land on the **Supervisor Dashboard** showing verification stats, teacher progress, and pending reviews.

## Step 2: Monitor teacher exam entries

The dashboard shows which teachers have entered exams and which are missing.

1. Click **Verifications** in the sidebar.
2. You'll see a table of teachers with:
   - Class and subject assigned
   - Month and exam type
   - Number of students entered vs. expected
   - Completion status

Teachers marked "incomplete" haven't entered results for all students.

## Step 3: Verify exam results

When a teacher submits exam results, they need your approval.

1. Click **Verifications** in the sidebar.
2. Review the pending exams.
3. For each entry:
   - Check the scores look reasonable
   - Verify the correct students are included
4. Click **Approve** to publish the results to parent reports.
5. Click **Reject** if something is wrong — the teacher must re-enter.

Approved results immediately appear in parent and student portals.

## Step 4: Review lesson plans

Teachers submit lesson plans for your review.

1. Click **Lesson Plans** in the sidebar.
2. You'll see plans in different statuses:
   - **Submitted** — awaiting your review
   - **In Review** — AI has scored it, pending your decision
   - **Approved** — published
   - **Rejected** — sent back to teacher

3. Click a plan to see:
   - The full weekly schedule with periods
   - AI review scores across 10 categories
   - AI executive summary and recommendations
   - Your comment field

4. Add your feedback and click **Approve** or **Request Revision**.

## Step 5: View exam reports

1. Click **Reports** in the sidebar.
2. Select a class and term.
3. View aggregated exam data across all subjects.

## Step 6: Take teacher actions

As a supervisor, you can also perform teacher-level actions:

- **Attendance** — record attendance for any class
- **Homework** — assign homework to any class
- **Quizzes** — create quizzes for any class
- **Grade Quizzes** — manually grade quiz attempts

These work exactly like the teacher workflow.

## What you built

You've completed the supervisor loop: monitor teachers → verify exams → review lesson plans → generate reports. You're the quality gatekeeper ensuring data accuracy and teaching standards.
